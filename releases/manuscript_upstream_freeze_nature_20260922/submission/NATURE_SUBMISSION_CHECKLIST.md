# Nature Article submission checklist

Checked against official Nature pages on 2026-09-22.

## Manuscript and scope

- [ ] Original conclusions are not published or under consideration elsewhere.
- [ ] Broad interdisciplinary importance is explicit in the first page and cover letter.
- [ ] Working title is at most 75 characters and avoids abbreviations/technical jargon.
- [ ] Fully referenced summary paragraph is at most 200 words.
- [ ] Main-text plan fits either ~2,500 words with 4 modest display items or ~4,300
      words with 5–6 display items.
- [ ] Main-text references are normally at most 50; one publication per number.
- [ ] Methods are concise, reproducible and normally at most 3,000 words.
- [ ] All authors approve the manuscript, order and contribution statement.

## Version and evidence gates

- [ ] Global identity/malignancy version is declared once and used consistently.
- [ ] V8 lineage-only evidence is not described as a global V8 reference.
- [ ] V9 `FROZEN_CANDIDATE` evidence is not described as a global frozen reference.
- [ ] V5-derived communication panels are rerun or explicitly labeled V5-derived.
- [ ] Sealed 12-GSE benchmark and promotion decision are documented.
- [ ] Copykat_python `genome='hg20'` is verified or excluded from final claims.
- [ ] Every result has an exact n, unit of analysis, denominator and source artifact.
- [ ] Every figure panel has a row in `figures/FIGURE_PANEL_SOURCE.tsv`.

## Statistics and reporting

- [ ] Methods define every statistical test, one/two-tailed status, covariates and
      multiplicity correction.
- [ ] Legends define exact n, biological/technical replicates and error bars.
- [ ] Exact P values are reported where relevant, including non-significant results.
- [ ] Nature Life Sciences Reporting Summary official PDF is completed in Adobe Reader.
- [ ] Human-data ethics, approvals, consent and secondary-use statements are verified.
- [ ] LLM assistance, if retained in the submitted work, is disclosed in Methods;
      an LLM is not listed as an author.

## Figures and Extended Data

- [ ] Main figures use Arial/Helvetica, 5–7 pt at final size, accessible colors and
      no red–green-only or rainbow encoding.
- [ ] Final main figures are editable vector/layered production files; review PDF is
      not the only source.
- [ ] Figures target 90 mm single-column or 180 mm double-column width and at most
      170 mm height.
- [ ] Legends are less than 250 words and understandable without the main text.
- [ ] Integral supporting data are assigned to no more than ten Extended Data items.
- [ ] Small supporting figures/tables are not buried in Supplementary Information.
- [ ] Source data and raw image requirements are satisfied where applicable.

## Availability and declarations

- [ ] Data Availability states how the minimum dataset can be accessed and lists
      accessions/restrictions/timeframes.
- [ ] Central custom code is available for peer review and has an archive/DOI plan.
- [ ] Funding statement, author contributions and competing interests are complete.
- [ ] Related manuscripts with overlapping authorship are disclosed and uploaded.
- [ ] Third-party figure/data permissions are documented.

## Initial submission files

- [ ] One line-numbered Word/PDF containing text and review-quality figures, preferably
      under 30 MB; figure legends are beside their figures.
- [ ] Supplementary Information contains only essential large/specialized material.
- [ ] `SIGuide.doc` is prepared for all SI files.
- [ ] Reporting Summary and any study-specific reporting forms are included.
- [ ] Cover letter is reviewed; confidential conflicts/related work are disclosed.
